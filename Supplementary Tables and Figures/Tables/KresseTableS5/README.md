# Table S5: 
List of the 35 extant species in our dataset. Limited to the western Atlantic Ocean above the equator. Proportional abundance and DCA scores for each taxon are given. The number of occurrences and the environmental variables associated with each taxon from the OBIS database are listed (mean, median, standard error, minimum, and maximum of latitude, bathymetric depth, salinity, and sea-surface temperature). 

## Note on filtering OBIS fields:
* **Depth**:	Used the bathymetry column for depth, excluding occurrences with negative bathymetry measurements
* **Geometry**:	Based on map of ocean boundaries (see attached and cited in manuscript), I followed the boundary given for the North Atlantic above the equater and west of the Mid-Atlantic Ridge, excluding the Gulf of Mexico. The latitude bounds are from 0 degrees to the Denmark Strait.
* **Fossils**:	Excluded occurrences where "basisOfRecord" column does not equal "FossilSpecimen"
* **Salinity categories**:	Marine is SSS >= 30 and brackish is SSS < 30
* **Manually cleaned fossil species**:	Anadara lienosa, Diplodonta nucleiformis, Lirophora latilirata
